
# General
The border city of **Vu'cut** stands as the **second border** town in between [[Palak]] and [[Sculum]] a city with many different meshing cultures meshing alchemy and ancient magics (these magics were imported through the Aviora scholars who visited a long time ago). A somewhat sandy town with places of folliage as one rises towards the top. 

**ITS A CITY OF CONNECTION. IT WAS ONE OF THE FIRST TRADE CITIES EVER ESTABLISHED BETWEEN SCULUM AND PALAK**
