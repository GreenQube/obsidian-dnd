## Dika - Victor du Mourir

### Backstory

Born into a Noble family du Mourir, Victor spent his early days "lavishly". The banquets and ballrooms were the earliest memories. That and growing up with multiple siblings. That is what he cherished the most. Spending time either destroying the gardens or listening to stories read by her elder sister. A balance of doing what people tell you not to and family. It was peaceful.

At a certain point his father Cardinal Gilian du Mourir was removed from his position and granted the title of Marquis of Doll Galley. For a poor man this is a fever dream but prestige comes with pride and once the scales lower on one side the other tends to tip over. His Mother, Lorien du Mourir wasn't a fan of the other noble's eyes looking from above, after all she was someone who never knew what it's like to be...lesser. Her family would in days like these use their ace in their sleeve. She couldn't lose her place so she had to bribe her way back and to whom but to a devil. The Greydoor's protector and benefactor. A lawyer and a bureaucrat. Xylaz.

At that time Victor was attending the Arcanum. A school for privileged but he felt well bored. Nothing seemed to draw his attention. That is until necromancy stepped in. Only basics were taught but the more cool stuff like getting someone back from the dead was a prohibited thing. Unless you are of the clergy. It seems that intellect has no place in raising the ones who passed.

So, after Lorien went through the staff of the Doll Galley she turned to her children. Their life would be extinguished in just a couple of nights. Ending with his fathers. Homecoming was no pleasant as it was empty and abandoned. The garden has been dug up. He found the remains of two members of his family. Desperate to try and get a sense of what he felt he used what was forbidden, he brought his sister and his brother back. But undead. His mother seemingly took other family members and dispersed them across the land and now he is trying to find all of his brothers and sisters, because if he achieves true lichdom, he can bring them back.

He roamed across the countries, doing jobs here and there. Components are not cheap and he can no longer go back to his old ways so it's just an adventure now. He practices what the oldest of his sisters, Celine, taught him. Kindness is to be shared. He practices what the oldest of his brothers Adrien, taught him. Keep to yourself but always be ready.

He is just weird. Good weird. And corpses are his friends.

### **Lore Things he Knows**

The great nation of **G'nawentus,** the largest of all of the recognized nations of **the Empire of Ar’ila** sits as the culmination of all of the smallest and ingenuine critters that came from all walks of life to join in a country that would celebrate them being small but together. The most diverse number of rodentfolk can be found here from hamsterfolk, ratfolk, mouse and squirrelfolk, to beaverfolk, or groundhogfolk. What sets the appart is their willingness to embrace feats of engineering and magical thinkering within items. Beaverfolk which build dams in circles of abjuration to reinforce them, rats which make burrows and passegaways not only through wood as their ancestors but working on planar travel to move between even the walls of the worlds. The nation of G’nawentus is a technocratic democratic socialist nation with its leaders chosen by a 1bilion vote system (named so for when the nation was founded, reportedly there were 1 bilion citizens). It has a dutchy which swears legion to them, The Dutchy of **Noglad (moles + hedgehogs).**

Of the G’nawentus citizens one must point out those infused with magic in their very dna ,how animalfolk have evolved through the centuries to have magic imbued in their very being, a now mostly dormant gene but some are born with the ability to unlock it.

In past, tales of ratfolk phasing in and out of pocket dimensions, hamsterfolk developing actual pockets of dimension in the folds of their mouths

Squirrel folk evolving folds which not only gave them the ability to glide but also fly, being infused with the magic of flight.

## Miljana - Zuko Buttercup (he/him)

### Backstory

Somewhere in the world, a great master dies and elsewhere a new baby is born. His parents always said he was a strange child. Born as one of the nine children, Zuko Buttercup was raised by a doomsday cult to prepare for The End. His village was full of paranoid conspiracy theorists who believed the world was going to end in a dozen different ways, so they obsessively collected every piece of trash they could find in the hope of survival.

Zuko was a master of recycling. He was well versed in the chaos and confusion of being a prepper and knew how to get through this life he was gifted. Further, while aspiring to be a good son, but avoiding his family as much as possible, our hero spent time in a local pub where he learned the ultimate skill: How to calm one's anxiety by achieving spiritual enlightenment and a strong skeletal system with just a glass of full-fat milk!

One day, while he was improving on his martial arts repertoire, along with trying to get some pocket money by faking injuries and threatening to sue, Zuko came across a traveling adventurer (a freaking cat!) who told him tales of a free world, beyond the village veil. Zuko was particularly on edge that day, and in a moment of pure bravery, decided to leave everything then and there and go find the thing he was always searching for. A place of peace and quiet.. warm milk.. and occasional freshly baked cookies with chocolate chips. As a memento and to keep him safe on the journey, traveler gave him a simple necklace with a little cat paw pendant attached. Very cute, very demure.

At the head of the family, the ruler is **Doran Ru;za** a grayfox of noteriety.
