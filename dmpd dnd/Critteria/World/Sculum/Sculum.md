**DESCRIPTION**
On the eastern shores of Ar'ila lay one of the oldest nations, Sculum. As one of the oldest and most prolific nations they used to span over all of the world as their ancestors were giants in their own right. They are the oldest and one of the richest empires in the lands, and they use that age and money wisely, investing in foreign inventions, scholars and mages. They are known for laying some of the foundations of magical teachings and learning of magic circles. They have since leaned more towards what could be classified as alchemical and chemical enchantments, alongside **Palak**. If one would look towards astronomy they could find the Solar and Lunar lizard druids of Sculum, and if one would want to discover old tomes of magic and go to the roots of magical thinking and development they should find their way to Sculum.
In each **region**, **area** or big enough city there rests a leader, a **Montez**. A **Montez** answers to the King Supreme reigning in the center of the lands, usually named a **Teno** (tail of the god Serpent). 
These lands have over a dozen(~12) Montez and a Tano to which they bring monthly tithes. The Teno does not concern with how they run their regions or cities as long as they pay the fees.
In these lands, four prominents gods are worshiped, **Luska** the goddess of warmth/sun/fire represented by a snake coiling into a sun. **Sanara**, god of change/death/evolution, and two twin gods of spinning, **Ikiz**(love, reverly, good times, birth) and **Ler** (war, hunt, stalking).
Since they hatch from eggs, they are animalfolk which almost always have room or places for their brood, or egg to keep warm and prepare for hatching.
## Landmarks

In the center of the land is the great capital **Listar**.

For in these lands it is one thing to be lost in culture, and another, more frightening thing to get lost in the vast planes themselves.
