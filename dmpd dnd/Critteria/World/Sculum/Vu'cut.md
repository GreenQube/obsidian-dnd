#### **The Sun Square** / **Square Market**
In the very center of Vu'cut lies The Sun Square. In it different animalfolk gather everyday, and when the market is open, trade spices, bobbles, jewelry, fancy wears, silks, clothes and much more. It is said that it was made so that one could gaze at the center of it and bask in the light of the goddess **Luska**, much like the sun fills their life, to stand in the square is to be embraced by her. This is where, sometimes one can see the priests and followers of **Luska** perform their sunrise and sundown prayers. 
*Shop*:
**Sleezy Snake** - Bar

#### **Tower of Light**
A little ways to the south, about 200m, to the **Sun Square**, built of dazzling marble, polished sandstone and colored glass, looking down on it as a keeper and reminder of the goddes of Light. In **The Tower of Light** the High Priest of **Luska** resides, as well as his sun disciples. It is said that entering the tower fills one with warmth of the spirit, mind and body. Blessed are those who enter, and protected are those who leave. In its **courtyard glass garden** one can bask in the rays of gold, or pray and be thankful to the warmth. Even in winter, this places stays a place of warmth.

#### **The Montez Rise** and **Yellow Houses**

North of the **Sun Square** inlaid with trees of *Ceiba* sits **The Montez Rise**. Built upon blocks of rock, iron, wood and stone sits a large yellow and white colored housing made to run, jury and lead the town of Vu'cut. Entering it often would be foreign travelers offering something to the city, connection to other merchants, or inventors of some kind. In it resides the local **Montez**, the city mayor. He is not seen much outside of it, but when he is, it is often in the company of the Yellow Jackets, the guarding force of this city. Wearing gold yellow jackets with green seems and padding these Yellow Jackets operate out as the cities protectors, guards and most importantly, brood defenders in their **Yellow Houses** (much like a police/guard station).

#### **Leechers Wing**
East to the **Montez Rise** we find the **Leechers Wing**, a part of town in servence of the goddess **Sanara**. *The floss silk tree* sprouts throught this district, embalming its lines around the housing and buildings. The medical wing of the city boasts medicine and ailment helping shops, services of healing, spa's, tea houses, as well as establishments known as **Broodes** which work as guard stations but as well as public warming grounds for eggs. It is not a shame nor shunned in these parts that an animalfolk with many of young ones on the way, with no protection or guarantee would leave their child in the **Broods** as they would see it, they are doing their necessary part to the cities and the nations betterment. As such, some children grow up in these **Broods** never knowing their parent, but muchever, glad to be under the Sun.

#### **Fur Den**
Yet, on the far south of the city, behind and to the west of the **Tower of Dawn**, shaded in mahogany and birch, shaded in cooling leaves of green and brown is the place known locally as the **Fur Den**. A place made by the people wanting to live and assimilate to Vu'cut and its many beauties. What's more it has become a valuable place if one would want to find someone to do an odd job, but a place where also a lot of visiting folk from other nations like to stay at. What makes it a bit dangerous to live in is the upcoming gang that has been sighted working in that place, but... thats a story for another time... Once a relatively simple even lawless place, Vu'cut was under the rule of Dana Katush. An imposing emperor flat lizard whose policies were made to extract as much of the resources of the people he sought to serve. He placed great taxing on people coming through and did not care for the populace to survive. Until one day, a group of 5 people (led by his son Varian) and a small army, at the end of the festival of the Sun usurped the throne and Varian killed his father. By that right, he took his place as the Montez and made sweeping changes. Opened the boarders as well as worked on reinstating the Tower of Light. He made hatcheries as well as instituted guards to protect the city from outside monsters attacking. Made trade easier as well as funded the White Lantern District. Ran colorful beaming festivals.
