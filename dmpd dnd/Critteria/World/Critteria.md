## Ar’ila ( Forest of Criteria)

### World Description

Nestled in the forest of Critteria, lies a great empire of Ar’ila ruled by the one called Critterian Emperor. Ar'ila is made up of multiple official nations, such as the nation of Aviora, a cunning kingdom of avian animals in which reside a plethera of smaller houses and noble lines, all answering to the king of Aviora, who in turn answers to the Critterian Emperor. One must not forget the ingineous and prosperous society of G'nawentus, a land populated by different types of rodent and small beasts alike, all proud to be bigger than life. However small in size they make up for in numbers and diverity. Alongside them one must not forget the emporium of Sculum, a society of reptilian religious creatures with an odd perspective to life, as well as Nessia, the ever zelous land of religious Fish people. Who know what these forests and lands hide more, of which smaller animal groups do they offer respite to.

Alongside these nations the forest is populated by all sorts of dangerous creatures, beasts, deamons and fiends. One must be careful not to stray too far away from their homes, lest they find themselves in the real wild of the Criterian forest.

  

## World Places

###  Ar’ila

An empire which has a ruler called the Critterian Emperor. A long standing tradition that Criteria ought to have a ruler and a representative of the lands stands. In the great city of Anuman resides the emperor. The current emperor is a renown scholar and a man of great renown, Sir Roland Galantys, (an Indian Peafowl) which has been ruling for quite some time, nearing an old age. The next Criterion Empire is chosen by the current ones wishes, usually chosen in on of the old ways, weather himself choosing an heir or a respected connected animal chosen by the people. The current emperor may choose in which ways he will be succeeded, but he must abide by the wishes of the other great nations that make up this empire.

Nations that make up the empire:

- G'nawentus (rodentfolk + allied land critters)
    
- Aviora (birdfolk)
    
- [[Sculum]] (reptilefolk - crocodiles, snakes, lizards, turtles, and tor- toises)
    
- Palak (amphibianfolk)
    
- Vulpera (foxfolk, weaselfolk, racoonfolk, etc)
    
- Locathia (sea fishfolk) + Nessia (land fishfolk)
    
- Bemur (lemursfolk + batfolk + others)
