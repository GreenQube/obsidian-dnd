On the eastern shores of Ar'ila lay one of the oldest swamp and forest nations, Palak. They are one of the oldest countries as well, but were problemed with civil inwars. They had a long history of the lands spliting into different waring areas which were held by armies led by either aristocratis in that time or organized militia wanting to take land. They are known for their expertiese in general ranger, druidic and swamp combat and movement. They are known as stealthing and people expertising in fast, dexterous quick combat, often "frog blitzing" an army. They have since leaned more towards what could be classified as alchemical and chemical enchantments, alongside **Sculum**. Through history they incorporated a lot of the alchemical and magical practices but more towards creating potions, poisons, illusion and adaptation magic as well as group magical warfare.
Since the **Great Toad Wars** one Frog Leader rose and united the clans and regions, some by defeating them, some by allying them up. Today, he is more of presentation figure of power, attending meetings and making grand decisions, but he lets his regents of his areas discuss among them.
In these lands, three most important gods lay, they share one with [[Sculum]],  **Luska** - fire/sun/warmth, a god of general health most commonly can be associated with the wildmother or mother nature - **Dathu** which represents homeostasis/health/nurture/skin, and the silent lord of the everlasting dark **Dhara** the god of revelry/arts/poison.
Since they hatch from eggs, they are animalfolk which almost always have room or places for their brood, or egg to keep proper for hatching
