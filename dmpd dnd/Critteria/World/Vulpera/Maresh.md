## General
A city of skunks, ferrets and foxes mainly. The second largest city in Vulpera, it was known as the city of hospitality and stay. There is a story of a lot of legendary creatures staying in the different Maresh inns and making their mark in different hidden spots in the city. As such it is believed that about 40 percent of the city is not what it seems with its different marks in the city.
A walled city with tall magick mushrooms growing from the top in the northern western side of the city.
