Vulpera is known for its cunning citizens as well as their adaptability and cultural fondness of athletic ability and political scheming. Of its many towns and communities 2 major cities stand out. _**Maresh**_ (city of badgers, otters and the like) and **Krata** (the capital city of mostly foxes, weasels, ferrets). *Krata, and by extension **Vulpera** has been ruled by the Ruza dinasty a family of foxfolk. At the head of the family, the ruler is **Doran Ru’za** a **grayfox** of noteriety.

**DESCRIPTION**
On the north western lands of Ari'la lays the the Kingdom of Vulpera. A relatively new kingdom which was built on the lands of multiple aincent lands containing different courts. There were once 10 courts. A land of deception and cunning with each of the courts ruling for themselves. Dangerous monsters, beings adapting to the wild and chaotic magic permeating in the air around it. As such even the denizens have had to adapt to those ways of live. A newer kingdom but chaotic in its roots the people have always been used to adapting to something which may not present itself in a certain way. In certain parts of the Kingdom one can find Layline Trees which spread and procreate thoroughout the land, somewhere poping out like vines, or making lesser laylines. Such places a font of power and originate and brim with power. The very essence of life and magic in such a place fuels the nature around often spawning different elementals, creatures and even making the plants and bugs come alive. The place is marked by hundreds of castles, homesteads and surrounding villages which do their best to protect themselves.
In such a chaotic nation what offers order, safety and protection are the 10 grand laylines protected by sacred protectors (Dogs).
