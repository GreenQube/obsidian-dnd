##  General 
**VIBES**
Scotish, Edinburgh perhaps. More like the main Capital city in Throne of Glass

**DESCRIPTION**
The city is protected by large, white walls and floored with cobblestone streets. A busy city of foxfolk, weasels and skunk, this beaming city of deals, trades and signage came to be much before our current time. Once a local lords castled town, this is the birthplace of Vulperas change for the better, for a connection to the world. This is the capital from which the first Court lord of the Roza family extended his reach into the realm and by uniting other courts to his cause, he helped build the very kingdom of Vulpera.

Used to be a poorer neighbourhood with good people, lot's of small shops that run, a very mellow communicative place where farmers would gather as well as lower tier merchants, cooks and forgers. Now it's a hotspot for illegal gambling and **fighting**. Now there's poor people running through, trying to find their way, food not being sent or made there much. Most of the resources going towards the Castle and the new "Guards".

There is a fighting pit called **Fighting Pits**. The fighting Pits have a nightly tournament where one can enter and if they go through the pit and win, they can pick a gallery piece of their choosing from the old Villa where the Fighting Pits are stationed. The fighting pits home (home that was made into the fighting pits) was a remade home of a member of the Roza Family (this used to belong to a friend of Doran, the previous leader of the kings guard, and a good friend of Doran, Alira knew about him, his name was Brodin).
