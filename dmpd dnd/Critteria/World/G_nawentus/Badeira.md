## General
Medium velicine grad **dabrova**. Grad na vodopadu naprevljenom od reke Bera, okruzen sumama, prelep grad koji je olicenje kroz storiju. Poznat po svom ekspertnom radu s drvecem, kao i po svojoj elitnoj familiji vojnika, boraca i granicarima **Dentes** porodici poznatoj po pravljenju **Drvogvozdane** jedinice ratnika koja je sacinjena iskljucivo od dabrova koji su radili vremenom na svojim razlicitim stablima.
Takav dabar ratnik od malena vezba i radi na tome da postane veliki ratnik idevsi od **obicnog stapa -> gvozdenog oruzja -> Drvogvozdanog stabla** koje on sam izabere iz Bele Sume (suma drvogzvozdanih stabala). Potom, ratnik oblikuje svoje stablo kroz vezbu, trud i rat kao i jacanje svojih zubi i delje sam svoje oruzje koje je jace od celika.

Jedino sto prate od bogova je neko koga zovu **Bela Dama (White Lady)** po cistoj vodi koja tece niz ogromnu reku **Bera**. 
Oko donjih delova gradova, pri prilazu gradu kao i po stranama grada mogu se videti kompleksi kuca i zgrada koje vode sto blize vodi da se koristi tok vode za **vodenice** sto im daje da ga koriste kao pseudo farme van grada, i **gvozdjarske**, **papirnate**, **drvoradske** i za **tening**.

## Landmarks
**Shanty Town / Intro Town**
A part of town which houses most **farmers**, **woodchoppers**. They live here purely, go and work on different farms weather in main town, in the mills outside where the produce for the town is made. Here a small shrine can be seen to the **White Lady**. The deity of these lands which is also a river spirit.

**Bridge of Trade - Bridge Corona**
A bridge that dates back to when this was a town was only forming, and the beaver people found that they needed more resorces in order to establish a town which sat atop cliffs and a waterfall. As such the Bridge of Trade was created. Various stalls, shops of trinkets, foods, and shops can be found (barber, clothing, hats, decor, etc.). As of late it has declined in its authenticity for the borders have been closed to Vulpera and with it other approaches to this town are difficult

**Main Town - Dentina Town**
Main town is where the Woodcarver guild resides, alongside various woodworkers, ironworkers and **woodruners**. The Main Town consists of two districts.
- **The guilding district** (working district)
	It consists of a couple of shops and workshops, and an artisan guild. This guild members are made from the members of these shops as well as people looking to learn more of these crafts. The shops all use powering by a deep basement mechanical segment which is connected to their very own watermill down the basin of the main town island. The shops in question are 
	- Fire Breath (vatreni dah) - blacksmith sells magic items made of wood and iron
	- Enchanting Branch (zacarana grana) - runecarving smith who enchants weapons
	- Mimic (mimik) - furniture store which makes special items with hidden compartments. Works closely with the **Creeping Vine** 
	- Creeping Vine (puzavica) - vehicle and general woodworking constructing tools.

- The rooting district (living and religious district)
	Consists of mostly well off houses which are most of the time one story houses. There is a beautiful one story sanctuary, a church of sort led by priests of the White Lady. They are not actual clerics but keepers of the place. They are mostly paladins of the Wild (using mostly water) 
	One can also find a teahouse.

**Dentes Training Grounds - Pulpa Grounds** 
Going from the connector path to the right, there exists a large military complex with housing used to train new recruits. It is a series of houses, towers shacks and different areas for training. As well it is adorned with a smithing house. To this extent, anyone recruited here either lives in the housing pits, or travels there although this is unlikely.

**Border (and border patrol) - Esmalte border**
To get to the top border there lies a gating office which is followed with faries which lead from and to the top of the waterfall. It can house up to 50 people (which is a lot). afterwards on the top there is a checkout point which leads from the walled and bridged path to land on either side of the river.

**Connector**
Connecting bridge to the Border, Dantes family complex Villa as well as the Dantes Training grounds. A wide bridge used for travel and connecting purposes, selling on it and using it other than for its functions is prohibited.

**Ironwood Forest**
Behind the Town there lies the Ironwood Forest. One of the most prominent forest around in which **woodchoppers** work. They are special trees which can withstand incredible heat and workings. The forest almost always shines in a silvery gray color with streaks of brown barely emanating (oxydized iron). For its name one can also find Copper, Aluminum, Manganese(Mangan) trees. One can even find special Gemstone trees, from which rare special weapons can be made. They are rare and cannot be cultivated outside of the forest by force.

## History and Culture
**General**
It is believed that the great city was built by their ancestors centuries ago to ward off from would be attackers, predators and enemies. The great river **Bera** is viewed as a great force of the safety as well as deterent against attackers. This town has seen plenty of hardships in the past but always survived as a protecting city. It has a deep rooted culture of RESPECT and CARE for anything one could own. Whether that's their furniture, house, weapon or wood. They are really prideful and respect tradition. Known for their lineage of warriors known as the **Ironwoods** as well as their general knack for crafting, and woodenchanting. 
They are also very prideful of their teeth. Moreso for whoever has great teeth they are revered as great, but those with less fortunate history tend to not be looked at favorably. Thus they rank them from bad(chipper == kvrcko) - normal - amazing - Dentes.  

**Perils**
Local wildlife critters such as termites and other bugs eat away at the very produce they pride themselves off so they are always looking to defend their farms. It is very difficult and oftentimes laborious. Furthermore poachers or outside influence want these trees and their special properties and while they do provide some to G'nawentus that doesent mean they provide as much as they'd like.
Water waste is a thing and if not handled properly with different laques and gels can undo anything one does. The river while merciful, can also be dreadful in times of hard rains.
There are also sea critters, elementals and other things that can come up from top of the river or from below if one is not careful. 
Fires are seen as scarier than night.

**Joys**
Their oreWoods give them different material wood allowing a skilled artisan to work with something with strength of iron but the elasticity and pliability of wood. Thus great pride is installed in every work they do when done properly. They are not cheats nor scammers and they are filled with pride from all of the work they do. The great river **Bera** provides ample foods and protection as well as always flowing water. Food and water are almost never a problem and the place is beautiful when winter comes. Alongside that they are one of the greatest warriors which date back about 500 years minimum. The Dentes family has always kept them good clean fed and under shelter and the people adorn them.
