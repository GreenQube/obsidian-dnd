### Backstory

**Mashak's Backstory**: A Zealot of the Sun-Serpent Mashak was born in Vu'cut. Specifically in ‘Chinatown (name pending). 
This was a tightly-knit community of mice, a vibrant, bustling district. From his earliest days, Mashak was raised in the faith of Luska, the Sun-Serpent. To his people, Luska was not just a goddess of warmth and fire, but the very embodiment of life's vital energy, the coiled power that brought forth the sun each day. They saw her as a great serpent, her scales shimmering like the desert sun, her body wrapped in an eternal embrace around the fiery orb. 
Mashak's parents were devout followers. His father was a scribe, meticulously copying ancient scrolls that spoke of Luska, and his mother was a healer, using her knowledge of herbs and the sun's energy to mend the sick. 
Mashak's zealotry began early. He was a bright, eager child, absorbing the teachings of the temple with fervor. He excelled in his studies, memorizing the sacred texts, and performing the rituals with a passion that bordered on fanaticism. He saw the world in stark terms: the radiant glory of Luska versus the encroaching darkness. He yearned to spread the goddess's light, to purge the shadows wherever they might linger. 

Currently, Mashak resides in Vu'cut, striving to alleviate suffering and spread Luska's light in his own way. He dedicates himself to helping those in need, regardless of their size or social standing. Whether it's mediating disputes between squabbling neighbors, tending to the sick with his limited healing abilities, or simply offering a comforting presence to the downtrodden, Mashak approaches each task with unwavering dedication. In addition to his direct aid, Mashak regularly ventures to Vu'cut's town square. There, he stands upon makeshift platforms and preaches the word of Luska to anyone who will listen. He speaks of the Sun-Serpent's radiant power, the importance of faith and community, and the promise of a brighter future for those who embrace Luska's light. His fiery sermons often draw a mixed crowd, ranging from curious onlookers to devout followers, and sometimes even skeptical dissenters. For now, Mashak remains in Vu'cut, believing that his work there is far from finished. He feels a strong connection to the city and its people, and he is committed to continuing his efforts to spread Luska's teachings and make a positive impact on the community.
