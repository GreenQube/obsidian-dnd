## Pjer - Jaque
### Backstory

The Rosenmont family is renowned as one of the finest candle makers in the world of Critera. They live on a high hill alongside a tribe of moles, with whom they share a harmonious life. Alongside candle making, they practice the ancient art of rune carving, a skill that has strengthened their bond with the moles over generations. Now, the time has come for Jaque and his eight siblings—**Hugo, Léon, Camille, Amélie, Chloé, Élise, Lucille**, and **Maëlle**—to leave their home in search of self-discovery. According to the family tradition, each young hoglet must venture into the world, unable to return home for at least a year. This rule is part of the Rosenmont legacy, as is their commitment to both spiritual and physical enlightenment. Each sibling has been trained in martial arts by their grandfather, Benoît, whose wisdom and skill are legendary. As they set off, their parents, Élodie and Lucien, bid them farewell, and so begins Jaque's grand adventure. His personal journey of self-discovery involves finding every scent the world has to offer. Standing at three feet tall, Jaque wears a light blue shirt under a black suit. Roses adorn some of his spikes, adding a unique charm to his appearance. He has shoulder-length curly hair, hazel eyes, and a captivating smile. Always by his side is the cane his grandfather gifted him—a symbol of strength and a reminder of home.
