### Backstory
The Pattern-Seer
Born in the secluded scholarly commune in the misted swamps of Palak, Nika Starshade was raised beneath silver moons and weeping willows. Her family belonged to the Order of the Eclipse, a reclusive sect of frogkin stargazers, lorekeepers, and pattern-readers. Her mother Velara is a revered moon-mathematician and matriarch of the order, known for calculating precise seasonal timings to guide agriculture and diplomacy. Gentle but strict, she taught Nika the discipline of cycles: nothing occurs without precedent, and everything returns. Her father Cain is a cartographer and tide-seer. He charted river dreams and wrote poetry no one understood. Nika is the only child born under a lunar eclipse - considered a sign that she’d be both watcher and breaker of cycles.
From a young age, she noticed things - misplaced moss, lingering glances, silence stretched too long. While others heard croaks and wind, she heard patterns. But one day, something happened - a flood, a fire, a vision - the records are unclear. 
What is known is that she appeared at the palace gates soaked to the bone, silent, and carrying a scroll. Within days, three of her predictions proved true. One whispered warning saved a diplomat’s life. One broken pattern she mentioned brought down a spy.
So the court gave her a tower and a title - The Pattern Seer. And now she watches. Listens. Waits.
