Backstory: 
 
For decades, Mr. Hoppings was the most sought-after tutor among noble families. He is strict, yet kind, a teacher who believed in discipline and wisdom, encouraging his pupils to achieve the greatest potential. He didn’t just teach, he molded minds.

His most prestigious job came when the esteemed Thumperton family hired him as a live-in tutor for their prodigious young heir, Flopsy. The Thumpertons were wealthy, generous, and beloved.But their influence caused envy. And Flopsy was a brilliant young wizard.

Then came the assassination.

The Thumpertons’ enemies struck in the night. Hoppings saw it happen, but was helpless despite being an excellent wizard. The weight of failure crushed him. He had taught Flopsy to unravel magic, but not how to survive the dark. He was recognized worldwide for his power, but could not use it to protect his pupil.

He retired from his tutor life, and he now runs a modest potion shop in Vu’cut, selling remedies to common folk.

Mr Hoppings didn't have many friends, but he Spirou Feorag has caught his attention. Hoppings has seen many knights but nobody as brave and loyal as he was. They have quickly built strong friendship and went on adventures together. After a decade, they decided they wanted something else in life, and they parted way but still considered eachother very close. 

When Spirou died, Hoppings felt true sorrow for the first time. That is why he feels very close with his grandson who has the same name. He is nicer to him than the rest, despite not knowing much about him.
