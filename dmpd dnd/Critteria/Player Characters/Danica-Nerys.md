### Backstory

In the lush, sun-dappled woods of the Eldergrove, Nerys was born into a tribe of cunning vulpins, where trickery and cleverness were valued above all else. From a young age, however, Nerys felt a different calling. While her kin reveled in mischief and deceit, she possessed an unwavering sense of justice and compassion. Her heart swelled with a desire to protect the vulnerable and uphold the balance of nature, traits that set her apart in a society that thrived on clever subterfuge.

As a result, Nerys was often the target of ridicule. Other vulpins, quick to mock her ideals, would tease her with scornful laughter, calling her "the fool with a heart." Though deeply hurt, she remained steadfast in her principles, refusing to compromise her values. Eventually, the taunting became unbearable, and after a particularly harsh encounter, Nerys made the heart-wrenching decision to leave her tribe in search of a place where she could belong.

Her journey was one of solitude and reflection. Nerys wandered the wilderness, feeling the weight of loneliness but also the invigorating freedom of being true to herself. One fateful day, she stumbled upon a secluded monastery nestled in a grove, where a gentle turtle named Miriel served as the main priest. Miriel, wise and compassionate, welcomed Nerys with open arms, sensing the turmoil within her heart.

At the monastery, Nerys found solace. She devoted herself to the community, helping with chores, tending to the gardens, and learning the ways of nature. Miriel became a mentor to her, teaching her the importance of balance, healing, and the strength found in humility. It was here that Nerys began to hone her innate connection to the natural world and discovered the teachings of the Oath of the Ancients. Inspired by the beauty of life and the magic that flowed through the land, she felt her calling deepen.

As the seasons changed, the monastery became a sanctuary for knights of the Order, who came to seek guidance and share tales of their noble deeds. Nerys watched in awe as they stood as protectors of the innocent, driven by a strong sense of duty. Their bravery ignited a fire within her; she yearned to join their ranks and make a difference in the world.

After years of service, Nerys approached the Order with determination. With Miriel’s blessing, she pledged her Oath of the Ancients, vowing to protect the natural world and those who could not protect themselves. The knights, impressed by her resolve and unwavering spirit, accepted her into their fold, despite her being the only fox among them.

Now, as she embarks on her first mission, a task that leads her to the bustling streets of Roden, Nerys carries the hope of her past and the strength of her newfound family. She aims to protect the vulnerable and prove that even the most unlikely hero can forge a path of light in a world that often shrouds itself in darkness. With her heart as her guide and the teachings of Miriel echoing in her mind, Nerys is ready to face whatever challenges await her, determined to show that justice and kindness are powerful forces in their own right.

### Lore things she knows

Vulpera is known for its cunning citizens as well as their adaptability and cultural fondness of athletic ability and political schemeing. Of its many towns and communities 2 major cities stand out. **Maresh** (city of badgers, otters and the like) and Krata (the capital city of mostly foxes, weasels, ferrets). **Krata**, and by extension **Vulpera** has been ruled by the **Ruza dinasty** a family of foxfolk. At the head of the family, the ruler is Doran Ru’za a grayfox of noteriety.

Of the Vulperan citizens one must point out those infused with magic in their very dna ,how animalfolk have evolved through the centuries to have magic imbued in their very being, a now mostly dormant gene but some are born with the ability to unlock it.
