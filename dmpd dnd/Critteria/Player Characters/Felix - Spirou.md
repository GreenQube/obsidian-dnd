### Backstory
Spirou Feorág 
![[clip_image002.jpg]]
  
Age: 24  
Species: Eurasian Red Squirrel

Class: Fighter  
Subclass: Knight errant (llama)

Spirou comes from a noble lineage and has grown up around wealth  
and etiquette. Being the oldest son of house Feorág from a young age, he was being prepared to become  the leader of his house. Learning  to read, use a sword and the ride a hare (horse? Idk what could count as  a mount, would love a badger tho).  One day, on a dry summer’s night, tragedy struck. As if smited by god himself, the family estate went up in flames. Only a handful of servants and Spirou survived. That night Spirou took his steed, Fantasio, on trip around the forest surrounding the estate, something he did quite frequently. Due to the thick shrubbery, he only noticed the fire and the smoke too late. His family’s estate and wealth had burned down to the ground. His parents, his little sister...burned alive, their corpses unrecognizable, however they were not among the survivors. During the fire he ran into the burning tree house to look for his family. Now the only surviving member of his house, Spirou set out to make a name for himself. To live up to his father’s legacy and protect the realm from ...... (possible threat).  
  
Ambitions:

·         Live up to his father’s ideals of knighthood, protect to weak no matter the cost

·         Rebuild the house of Feorág or let its name be etched into the story of the world

Strengths:

·         Strategically clever, can hold his own in a fight

·         Kind, sometimes to the point of naïvity

·         Wants, needs to help the helpless

Weaknesses:

·         His family’s legacy weighs heavy on him, unsure whether he is the one that should have survived à insecurity

·         Has a fear of fire, gives him flashback to seeing the flames consume his home/family. He hears the screaming of his friends and family. He sees his family burn to a crisp in bonfires big enough to burn things down (wisdom saving throw?)
