#### Pantalaimon, the Quiet Flame
Among the silver-glassed trees of the frozen north, Pantalaimon was once just a name whispered between siblings and snowflakes; a gentle soul, white as the winter that bore him, living a quiet life of warmth and familial closeness. His words were few, but his presence spoke volumes. Reserved but dependable, he was the one who would stay behind to watch over the den while the others played, the one who always noticed when someone was hurting but didn’t know how to ask for help.

That peaceful world was shattered in one night of fire and screaming.

The warband came like a nightmare made real, a cult of dark zealots devoted to destruction. Pantalaimon had wandered out for one of his habitual, solitary naps, curling into a crook of a tree beneath a shaft of moonlight. When he returned, everything was gone. His kin, his home, and most painfully - Auriel, his mate and dearest confidante - vanished without a trace. Some say she was taken. Pantalaimon refuses to hope, and yet, in the quiet, his heart still listens for her voice in every whisper of wind. The only keepsake of her is the silver charm shaped in a star he always keeps in his breast pocket.

In the wake of that loss, he did not cry out. He did not scream. He carried his grief inward, locking it behind the armor of discipline and silence. It is this same quiet intensity that led him to the **Oath of Devotion**. Not for retribution, but for protection. For justice. For the belief that even in a world cold and cruel, there must be light.

Pantalaimon, now a paladin, walks a path forged in silence and sorrow. He speaks little, but when he does, his words carry a calm gravity that silences rooms. He radiates a quiet strength, emotionally self-contained, almost unreadable, but his actions speak of profound loyalty. He will stand between danger and his companions without hesitation, not because he was ordered to, but because it is right.

There is an intensity to him, the kind that burns slow and steady. He will stay up on watch long after others have fallen asleep, not because he distrusts them, but because he can’t bear the idea of losing someone again. His affection is subtle - shared silence, a light touch of the tail when someone is in pain, a cloak draped over a sleeping friend. He rarely shows vulnerability, but when he does, it's raw and unfiltered, like a crack in the ice revealing the deep, still water beneath.

And though he often drifts off into naps - especially in sunbeams or warm corners - those brief sleeps are his escape from the heavy burden he carries. In dreams, he visits a world where his mate still laughs, where the snow is unbroken by blood, and where he is simply Pantalaimon - not a paladin, not a protector, just a creature.

He may appear aloof, but those who earn his trust find in him something deeper: a heart that still loves fiercely, a soul shaped by tragedy but not consumed by it. He will not say the words easily, but his actions will echo them every day:

“I will never leave you. Not again.”
