# Description 🙏🏼🤖

## Current

Currently, he finds himself straddling two very different worlds. By day, the young tiefling warlock lives in a vibrant, free-spirited commune **Hipidoz** on the edge of the city, where he tends to a flourishing garden and works with local plants to help sustain the community’s natural vibe. By night, he dons a very different role as a cook at the bustling **Disco Voz** in the city, where his culinary skills provide a warm, welcoming counterpoint to the urban chaos. His passion for old books—sparked by his pact of the tome—drives him into the city’s shadowy back alleys (e.g **Borivoja Gojkovića**), where he frequents secret, underground basement shops behind the wizard university **Pufdoz** in search of rare, forbidden tomes. All the while, the lingering influence of his enigmatic archfey patron colors his every step, imbuing his everyday life with a constant hint of otherworldly mischief and mystery.

## Backstory

**How It Began**

While roaming the wilds as an outlander, your tiefling found himself on the brink of starvation. Weak and disoriented from days without a proper meal, he collapsed in a secluded clearing where nature’s magic pulsed through every leaf and vine. In that desperate hour, he was unexpectedly saved—not by a burst of healing magic, but by an offering of food. A mysterious force provided a bountiful, sumptuous feast that nourished his body and rekindled his will to survive.

**The Unusual Gift**

After ensuring his survival, the unseen benefactor left him a curious, ancient book lying on the forest floor. At first glance, the book appears to be nothing more than an ornate cookbook brimming with exquisite recipes for rare and magical dishes. However, when the pages are turned to the opposite side, they reveal a completely different nature: the same book unfolds into a tome of cantrips and arcane knowledge. This duality hints at the whimsical and unpredictable nature of the patron, whose gifts blend the nourishing with the arcane.

**The Pact's Implications**

Now bound by this peculiar bargain, your warlock wields powers that are as unpredictable as they are practical. By day, his interest in the culinary arts—perhaps even an uncanny knack for cultivating and preparing rare herbs—reminds him of the feast that saved him from starvation. By night, the book’s other side grants him access to subtle but potent cantrips that serve as the foundation of his warlock abilities.
