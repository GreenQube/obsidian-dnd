Backstory of Seraphine Valraen, the Fallen Knight of Everdawn

  

Seraphine Valraen was born to a noble half-elf mother, Lady Elowen Valraen, and a human father, Sir Aldric Dain, a decorated knight in the service of the High Lord of Everdawn. Her childhood was shaped by both elven wisdom and human resilience. Trained in swordplay by her father and instilled with a sense of duty by her mother, she excelled in combat and strategy from a young age.

  

By the time she came of age, Seraphine had risen through the ranks of the Everdawn Knights, sworn defenders of the realm. She became one of the most celebrated warriors in court, her name spoken with admiration, her skill unmatched. She was not only a warrior but an inspiration—a beacon of hope for the people. Among her many achievements, she fought side by side with her beloved husband, Sir Cedric Faelan, a fellow knight and strategist. Their love was one of strength and devotion, sealed when he gifted her his family's heirloom—a silver necklace adorned with an intricate pendant, a phoenix entwined with a sword, representing rebirth and unwavering resolve.

  

One fateful night, the High Lord of Everdawn sent Seraphine and her company on a mission of grave importance. A village on the kingdom’s border had fallen silent—no messengers returned, no trade flowed. Rumors spoke of an ancient evil awakening in the ruins beyond the village, a darkness long thought sealed away. The Everdawn Knights, including her husband, rode out under Seraphine’s command to investigate.

  

When they arrived, they found the village eerily quiet, untouched by war or disaster. It was too perfect, too still. In her confidence, Seraphine ordered her knights to spread out, believing a mere scouting party could uncover the truth. She failed to see the trap until it was too late.

  

The village was not abandoned. It was a feeding ground for an ancient, slumbering horror—a being bound by a fragile seal, waiting for the right moment to break free. When Seraphine and her knights set foot inside, their presence shattered the final wards. Shadows coiled from the ruins, eldritch magic erupting from the earth. The knights fought bravely, but the darkness was relentless, twisting reality itself. One by one, they fell.

  

Seraphine tried to rally her forces, but her strategy crumbled under the overwhelming power they faced. In her desperation, she refused to retreat, believing they could hold the line. It was hubris, and it cost them everything.

  

She saw her husband cut down before her eyes, his final words a plea for her to run. But she did not flee. She fought until she was the last one standing. She fought until the horror was sealed once more—this time, by her own hands. But it was a hollow victory.

  

Her order was gone. Her husband was gone. And it was her fault.

  

As she stood in the ruins of her failure, Seraphine made an oath beneath the blood-red sky. She vowed that she would never let such evil claim another soul. That she would hunt down and destroy those who prey upon the innocent. That she would find a way to undo what had been done.

  

With shaking hands, she took Cedric’s necklace from his fallen form. The silver had darkened, tainted by the lingering corruption, yet the phoenix remained—scarred but unbroken. She reforged it into her holy symbol, a constant reminder of the vengeance she swore and the love she lost.

  

Seraphine abandoned the name of Everdawn. She wandered the land, hunting monsters, crushing cults, and driving back the forces of darkness. But she also sought knowledge—for in the depths of her soul, she refused to accept that death was the end.

  

Years later, Seraphine found herself walking a path she never imagined. Though she still fought against the darkness, she had begun working with a necromancer—one who claimed to have the knowledge to restore life itself. She despised the undead, yet she could not ignore the hope burning in her chest. If there was even the slightest chance to bring Cedric back, she would take it.

  

Her quest for redemption and revenge now teeters on a knife’s edge. Will she find a way to bring her love back, or will she lose herself to the very darkness she swore to destroy?
