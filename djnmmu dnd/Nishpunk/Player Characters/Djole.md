# **Backstory**

In the fog-choked streets of Nispunk, there’s an alleyway that few people pay attention to, tucked between the whirling gears of clockwork machinery and the smoke of steam-belching factories. It’s here that Azrathis, known to most simply as "Azra" in passing whispers, came into the world—a world that had not yet seen the full extent of his serpent-born power.

Azrathis wasn’t always a name spoken in hushed tones; in fact, he didn’t have a name at all when he first slithered into the grimy black market of Nispunk. Born as a mere cluster of wriggling snakes, he was one of the many hatchlings smuggled into the city in a sealed glass jar. The merchant who had purchased him and his batch of brethren, a scoundrel known only by his shadowy dealings, was eager to unload them for a hefty price. They were not intended for display as oddities or for any noble's exotic menagerie—no, these snakes were destined to serve a more strategic purpose: to become elite guards for the fortress of Plavi Krak, the iron-walled center of power that controlled much of the city’s politics and industry.

But one thing the merchant hadn't counted on was that the jar wasn't as secure as it seemed. Azrathis, a creature born from cunning and survival instinct, found a crack. It started small—a single sliver between the glass and the cork—but it was enough. Azrathis slithered out of his prison, escaping into the dark crevices of the city.

While the rest of the batch of snakes were quickly sold to the city’s military elite, Azrathis was left to fend for himself. The city was a labyrinth, a pulsing center of gears, steam, and secrets. With his mind sharpened by his unnatural origins, Azrathis grew quickly, learning the undercurrents of Nispunk’s alleyways, rooftops, and shadows. He became a familiar face in the criminal world, carrying out small-time jobs—smuggling shipments of rare mechanical parts, bribing officials, and providing protection for those who could afford it. But despite his success, something gnawed at him. He remembered a symbol from the crates he had been smuggled in—a strange mark, a sigil that had meant something, but what?

His search for answers led him to a new life: a humble, if far less glamorous, existence as a worker in one of the city’s less reputable pubs. The clinking of mugs and the murmur of drunken patrons became his cover. Here, Azrathis did the manual labor, carrying heavy crates, ensuring supplies arrived, and sweeping the floor. But beneath the surface, he was always listening. The drunken ramblings of a patron, the whispered deals between smugglers, and the conspiratorial meetings in dark corners—this was where Azrathis’s true skills came to light. Every conversation, every snippet of information about shipping routes, guards, or the Plavi Krak fortress, was carefully cataloged in his mind. Each scrap brought him closer to understanding the origin of his batch.

His powers, hidden from most, were a terrifying force when awakened. When pushed to anger, his body would stretch and contort, growing to the size of a giant serpent, his skin becoming tough and impenetrable, his strength amplified tenfold. Few could match the rage of Azrathis, though it was rare that he ever showed such wrath. Azrathis was patient—an assassin who could wait years to strike, if need be. It took much to provoke him, but when it came, it was like a flood—a whirlwind of scales and fury, unraveling everything in its path.

But the city knew him as something far less terrifying. To most, he was a mere laborer, a part of the backdrop of Nispunk’s busy streets and steam-powered machines. And yet, beneath that quiet exterior, Azrathis’s heart burned with a singular obsession: to uncover the truth behind the crate mark and the mysterious forces that had created him. He would find the answers, even if it meant going through the highest echelons of power in the city.

For now, he remains silent, lurking in the shadows of the pub, biding his time. And when the moment comes for Azrathis to strike, it will be the city that learns why it is dangerous to underestimate a snake born of both cunning and fury.
